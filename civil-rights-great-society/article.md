# The Right to Vote Was Only the Beginning

The full editorial manuscript, *The Right to Vote Was Only the Beginning: Civil Rights, the Great Society, and the Limits of Reform*, is maintained in the private [research-articles-medium](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/research-articles-medium) repository.

This public directory remains its reproducible research companion. The [analysis code](analysis.py), [historical source transcriptions](data/), [notebook](model_walkthrough.ipynb), [eight figures](figures/), [result tables](results/), and [scientific tests](tests/) remain available here.

Earlier public manuscript revisions remain in this repository's Git history.
