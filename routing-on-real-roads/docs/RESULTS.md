# Results: straight-line against road distances

Generated by `routing-roads estimate` from `results/solves.csv` and the committed matrices. Every estimand, interval and decision rule is section 6 of [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), fixed before any route was solved. Costs are total road distance; a positive regret means routes planned on straight-line distances drive further on the road than routes planned on road distances.

Here each straight-line route is driven in its shorter direction on the road, with no new solve. *Same routes* is the share of ordered pairs of seeds whose straight-line plans have the same routes up to direction.
