## The definition-break rule

One rule (`review.flagged_change`): a labour-force break flag invalidates the change into the break quarter; a low-reliability or definition-differs flag invalidates every change touching the flagged quarter (ES and FR carry `d` from 2021-Q1); a GDP break flag on any regressor invalidates the row. The Bayesian likelihood always applies it; the classical review applies it in its `quality_screened` sample; the registered classical headline does not, and stays primary. The Dutch survey was phased in during 2021 without a flag; every headline row whose window touches 2020-2021 is excluded, so it enters no headline row.
