# The EU as a whole: a labour-force-weighted aggregate

Generated by `eu-okun aggregate` under [AGGREGATE_PROTOCOL.md](../AGGREGATE_PROTOCOL.md), registered before its data were frozen or any estimate was computed. Two-lag sums of the unemployment change on GDP growth, on the headline sample (2009-Q3 to 2026-Q2, windows touching 2020-2021 excluded). 95% synchronised block-bootstrap intervals (block 4, B = 1999, seed 20261029). Associations only.

**Reproduction.** The 27 country sums equal the case study's unemployment elasticities.

**Data check.** The labour-force-weighted rate of the 27 countries matches the official `EU27_2020` rate within 0.07 pp over 70 quarters; the correlation of their quarterly changes is 0.979. The EU growth rate is from the 2026-10-20 release, later than the frozen country series (2026-10-15).

## Reading

- The EU growth rate weights countries by GDP, and the aggregate also carries the correlation of shocks across countries. The aggregate sum therefore need not equal any average of the country sums. None of these numbers is causal.
