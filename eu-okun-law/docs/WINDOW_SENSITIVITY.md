# Pandemic-window sensitivity (post-hoc, not pre-registered)

Generated by `eu-okun window-sensitivity`. Added after an external review on 2026-10-19; the registered results are unchanged and remain primary.

The registered rules exclude an observation if any period of its variables lies in 2020-2021: quarterly local projections from t-4, monthly from t-12, the structural VAR from t-2. The change at the longest lag is measured from the level one period earlier, so that level can fall in 2021 while the observation is kept. The widened window (margin 1) also excludes those observations. The shift-share feasibility study has the same gap: its origins keep t-4 to t clear, but the lag-4 controls of the GDP residual scale use the level at t-5 (added after a second review on 2026-10-20).
