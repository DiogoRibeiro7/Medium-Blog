# Okun's law in hours, for every country, with and without the pandemic

Generated by `eu-okun hours` under [HOURS_PROTOCOL.md](../HOURS_PROTOCOL.md), registered before any full-sample elasticity was computed. The pandemic-excluded elasticities are the case study's and had been seen; they are descriptive here. Two-lag elasticities to GDP growth (sum of the coefficients on g[t], g[t-1], g[t-2]), quarters 2009-Q3 to 2026-Q2, 95% synchronised block-bootstrap intervals (block 4, B = 1999, seed 20261026). Associations only.

## Reading

- Supported P1 and P2a would mean that, with the pandemic included, labour input in hours kept following output and the adjustment moved to hours per worker. That is the pattern job-retention schemes would produce; paid absence and closures with continued pay produce it too, and the 2021 survey redesign also falls in the window. Nothing here identifies a cause.
- Hours and persons are national-accounts, domestic concepts; unemployment is a survey concept for residents.
