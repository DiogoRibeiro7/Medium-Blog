# A common-correlated-effects mean-group panel

Generated by `eu-okun cce` under [CCE_PROTOCOL.md](../CCE_PROTOCOL.md), registered before any CCE estimate was computed. Each country's two-lag regression of the unemployment change on GDP growth, on the headline sample (2009-Q3 to 2026-Q2, windows touching 2020-2021 excluded), is augmented with the cross-country averages of GDP growth (lags 0-2) and of the unemployment change (Pesaran 2006). Country sums are averaged without weights. P1 uses the mean-group t statistic; intervals and P2 use a synchronised block bootstrap over quarters (block 4, B = 1999, seed 20261028). Associations only.

**Reproduction.** The 27 plain OLS sums equal the case study's unemployment elasticities.

## Reading

- A CCE sum is the association with a country's own growth beyond what the cross-country averages absorb. Like the Bayesian factor model's sum, it is a different estimand from the plain sum, and neither is causal.

poetry run eu-okun cce --data-dir snapshots/eurostat-2026-10-15 --case-study docs/heterogeneity/elasticities.csv --bayes docs/bayes/country_estimates.csv --output-dir docs/cce --report docs/CCE.md
