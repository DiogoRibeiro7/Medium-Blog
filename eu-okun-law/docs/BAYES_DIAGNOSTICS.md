## 7. Limits

- Exact leave-one-out is computed in the local run only; the seed runs use the registered PSIS procedure.
- Six runs bound how often a verdict on its threshold flips; they do not give its probability.
- The calibrated scale is chosen from the prior predictive alone; it makes the priors narrower, not better informed.
- No specification identifies a causal GDP shock.
