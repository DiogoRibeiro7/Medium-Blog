# The Recession Ended; the Damage Did Not

The full editorial manuscript, *The Financial Crisis of 2008, the Great Recession, and America's Unequal Recovery, 2007–2019*, is maintained in the private [research-articles-medium](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/research-articles-medium) repository.

This public directory remains its reproducible research companion. The [source data](data/), [data preparation](prepare_data.py), [analysis code](analysis.py), [eight figures](figures/), [three result tables](results/), [notebook](model_walkthrough.ipynb), and [scientific tests](tests/) remain available here.

Earlier public manuscript revisions remain available through Git history.
