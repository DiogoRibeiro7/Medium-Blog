  liste en sus 2016-2024: +4.6 bn gross, +1.8 bn net, so rebates on it rose by 2.8 bn, 36% of the rise in all rebates
IGAS-IGF mission (July 2025): rebates EUR 3.2 bn in 2019 and 8.3 bn in 2023, on reimbursable spending of 36.5 bn (22.7%); safeguard clause over EUR 1.5 bn in 2023 and 2024; yearly list-price cuts 3.7% of net spending in 2012-2019, 2.9% in 2020-2023
  if the slower pace of list-price cuts ran for the four years 2020-2023, French list prices would end +3.4% above the path of 2012-2019 (rough: the share of savings in net spending read as a rate of price decline)
  IGF: keeping list prices high also raises distribution costs, since pharmacy and wholesale margins are set on the list price (sentence found in the text)
