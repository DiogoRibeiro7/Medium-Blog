5. The time account
==============================================================================================
Part 4: Portugal 839 days from EU authorisation to availability, EU27 597: 242 days longer
  Portugal's excess over the models of Part 4: price + AIC 194 days; size + distance + AIC + price 183; with the cancer-medicine price level 173
  approved processes in Infarmed's tracker: 42; mean days beyond the legal deadlines: Infarmed 262, negotiation 251, together 513; median together 372
A delay defers spending on the delayed medicines. Scale: if the first-year spending of a year's new medicines equalled the whole 2025 increase in hospital spending (EUR 250.3 million, an upper end), a delay of 173-242 days would defer EUR 118-166 million a year
  For a medicine the SNS agreed to pay for, the price is at most what the SNS judged its health effect worth; the health forgone during the delay is worth at least the spending deferred.
