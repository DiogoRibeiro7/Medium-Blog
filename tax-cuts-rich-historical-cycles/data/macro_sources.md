# Annual U.S. output and investment measures, 1964–2018

These two annual time series are from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis,
distributed as FRED tables (both pages accessed 2026-11-02, last updated
2026-09-30):

- [A191RL1A225NBEA](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/A191RL1A225NBEA):
  real GDP annual percentage change from the preceding calendar year.
- [A006RE1A156NBEA](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/A006RE1A156NBEA):
  gross private domestic investment as a percentage of nominal GDP.

The CSV preserves published rates to one decimal, 55 consecutive calendar
years, with no interpolation or smoothing. Gross private domestic investment
includes residential investment, nonresidential fixed investment, and
changes in private inventories. It is **not** identical to business
productive fixed investment, and its GDP share is not its real growth rate.
The real GDP measure is a growth rate, not a level or a counterfactual
output gap.

These are revised historical national accounts, not real-time vintages
known to policymakers. Comparing them with tax laws does not isolate policy
effects: monetary policy, recessions, oil shocks, trade, credit cycles,
industrial change, and many other interventions coincide with the dates.
Any visual alignment is descriptive; before/after average comparisons or
correlations are not credible causal estimates.
