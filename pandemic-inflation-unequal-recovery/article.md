# Inflation Fell; Prices Did Not

The complete editorial manuscript, *The Pandemic Shock, Inflation, and the Unequal Adjustment, 2020–2025*, is maintained in the private [research-articles-medium](https://github.com/DiogoRibeiro7/research-articles-medium) repository.
