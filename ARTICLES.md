## Reproducibility

Each article has its own directory, dependency requirements, tests,
publication figures, and machine-readable experimental results. The analyses use historical source transcriptions, synthetic data, or explicitly
hypothetical assumptions according to their documented scope. Model outputs
are not historical estimates unless a source and method establish them.

To reproduce an article, enter its directory and follow the commands in
its linked study README. The manuscripts are prepared for Medium; inclusion
in this index does not imply that they have already been published there.
