Lease Sale 244

Cook Inlet

The Proposed Outer Continental Shelf (OCS) Oil and Gas Leasing Program for 2012-2017 included one special-interest lease sale in the Cook Inlet Planning Area (Sale 244). This sale occurred June 21, 2017. The bid reading was live-streamed from this page.

Sep 21, 2026The Marine Minerals Administration sends to Hilcorp Cook Inlet, LLC, a letter acknowledging the lease relinquishments detailed in the Sept. 15 item, below.

Sep 19, 2024BOEM sends to Hilcorp a letter acknowledging the lease relinquishments detailed in the Sept. 17 item, below.
Sep 17, 2024Hilcorp Alaska, LLC, informs BOEM that it is relinquishing the rights to seven leased blocks in Cook Inlet. The leased blocks are: OCS-Y-02434, OCS-Y-02435, OCS-Y-02436, OCS-Y-02438, OCS-Y-02442, OCS-Y-02446, and OCS-Y-02447. The rights to all seven were acquired in Cook Inlet Oil and Gas Lease Sale 244, held in June 2017. Hilcorp retains the rights to eight Cook Inlet leases: seven obtained via Lease Sale 244; and one obtained via Lease Sale 258, held in December 2022.

May 18, 2017

The Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM) today announced it will offer approximately 1.09 million acres off Alaska’s southcentral coast in a lease sale scheduled for June 21.

Cook Inlet Oil & Gas Lease Sale 244 will offer 224 blocks toward the northern part of the federal Cook Inlet Planning Area for leasing. The blocks stretch roughly from Kalgin Island in the north to Augustine Island in the south.

The Final Notice of Sale (FNOS) and a copy of the Record of Decision (ROD) affirming the sale are available now. The FNOS and a Notice of Availability for the ROD will be posted tomorrow (Friday, May 19) in the Federal Register Reading Room; and published on Monday, May 22, in the Federal Register. 2013

Nov 27, 2013BOEM issues Sale 244 Area Identification decision (map included). The area identified by the Bureau consists of 224 blocks toward the northern part of the inlet, and covers approximately 1.08 million acres of seafloor, stretching roughly from Kalgin Island in the north to Augustine Island in the south. IMPORTANT: The Area ID does not indicate that a final decision has been made to hold the potential lease sale, or on its size. Prior to making these decisions, federal law requires that the bureau study the potential environmental impacts of oil and gas development in the area. Accordingly, this Area ID announcement will be followed by publication in the Federal Register of a "Notice of Intent to Prepare an Environmental Impact Statement," along with opportunities for public comment. The final decision on the size of the sale -- and whether to hold the sale at all -- will be based on these and other factors.

2012

May 1, 2012Public comment period closes on Lease Sale 244 RFI.
Mar 27, 2012BOEM publishes the Request for Information (RFI) - Cook Inlet Oil & Gas Lease Sale 244 in the Federal Register. This initial request asks industry, the public, and key stakeholders about geologic, biological, archaeological, subsistence and/or socio-economic conditions that might bear on potential leasing and development decisions. The RFI does NOT indicate a decision to lease in the Cook Inlet Planning Area. BOEM will not make a decision about any potential lease sale before finalizing the 2012–2017 OCS Oil and Gas Leasing Program, and determining whether there is adequate interest in holding this sale and conducting thorough environmental reviews.
