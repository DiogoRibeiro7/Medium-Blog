## Citation-year convention

Several verified references have different online-first and final issue dates. For instance, Abadie et al. (2015) appeared online in April 2014; Cattaneo et al. (2015) appeared online July 2014; and Cattaneo et al. (2020) appeared online in 2019. The existing bibliography uses **final issue years**. The year embedded in a DOI string is not evidence that the issue citation year is wrong.
