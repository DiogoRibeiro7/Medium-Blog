## Reproduce independently

```bash
python verify_advanced_results.py
python verify_advanced_results.py --recompute
```

The default mode uses the standard library to validate unique experimental settings, replication counts, stated effects, bias identities and meaningful probability ranges. The full mode requires numerical dependencies, launches every script in an isolated temporary directory and checks every CSV field to numerical tolerance `1e-8`.

## Statistical interpretation

- Part 12 separates predictive fit from treatment-allocation value. Policies are evaluated on held-out simulated subjects at a fixed treatment capacity; good prediction alone is not equivalent to a beneficial policy.
- Part 13's doubly robust orthogonalization and cross-fitting are evaluated under the script's specific nonlinear and linear nuisance designs. The Monte Carlo differences should not be generalized beyond the data-generating process without sensitivity analysis.
- Part 14 studies regularization and the posterior under an assumed Gaussian likelihood with known observation variances. Posterior concentration does not repair omitted confounding or establish causal identification.

## Scope limitation

Part 13 additionally stores `validation_results.csv`, generated by a separate validation workflow. This audit confirms reproducibility of the main `experiment.py` and `results.csv` pair only. The additional validation artifact remains for a separate audit; it is not implicitly claimed as reproduced.

Exact repeated-seed reproduction supports numerical integrity, not robustness to different seeds, alternative prior models, nuisance learners, positivity violations, selection bias or generalization to observational data.
