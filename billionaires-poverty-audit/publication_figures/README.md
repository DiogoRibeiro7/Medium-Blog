## Scope and interpretation

- The panel comprises **54 countries** using **Forbes 2026 billionaire counts by citizenship** (secondary transcription), with OWID/World Bank $30/day 2021-PPP poverty shares and WDI PPP GDP per person and population.
- **These figures do not reproduce the original social-media chart**, which reported 64 countries and a billionaire-residence measure. All relationships are descriptive, not causal.
- Fixed panel SHA-256: `db18001307fc89c77275f9d6147c23ce5f33b9e35e8da640bef82ce725b6fbda`.
- Figures follow one palette (blue, orange and aqua slots validated for colour-vision deficiency), carry their own title, subtitle and source line, and are rendered at 2,250–2,500 px wide for Medium.
