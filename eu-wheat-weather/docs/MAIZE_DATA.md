# Data: the grain maize panel

Generated by `eu-wheat maize-panel` from the crop snapshot `eurostat-maize-2026-10-26`, the harmonised region codes and the E-OBS weather over each region's maize season. It describes coverage only: no yield is related to any weather variable here ([METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), section 9).

## Summary

- **Panel:** 1898 region-years in 132 regions of 19 countries, harvest years 2010 to 2024.
- **Codes:** 102 no_match, 27 nuts1_single, 204 nuts2021, 23 recode.
- **Harmonised region-years, 2010-2024:** 3092, of which 2656 have a yield. Sources that disagree by more than 1%: 15.
- **Excluded region-years, in the order the rules apply (9.1):** no_area_or_production 62; area_below_1kha 1025; data_rule 39; region_below_10_years 68.
- **The data rule (8.2), by rule:** Q1 3, Q2 22, Q3 6, Q4 8; by country: AT 10, CZ 12, DK 3, IT 6, NL 7, SE 1. Every flag is in [maize/quality_flags.csv](maize/quality_flags.csv).
- **Countries with a single region in the panel** (no within-country variation for M1): none.
- **The season (GGCMI rainfed maize):** in the panel's regions, planting runs from 29 March to 12 May and maturity from 31 August to 7 October; 0 regions would plant before 1 March and start on it.
