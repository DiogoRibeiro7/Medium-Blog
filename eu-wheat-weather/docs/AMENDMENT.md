## Sample

- **M1-A:** 3436 region-years in 163 regions of 22 countries, harvest years 2000 to 2024.
- **The data rule (8.2):** of the panel's 3771 region-years, 249 are flagged (by rule, with overlaps: Q1 11, Q2 258, Q3 12, Q4 24, Q5 4). By country: AT 1, BG 4, CZ 120, DK 30, EL 52, FI 8, IT 15, NL 11, SK 8. Regions then below 10 years and dropped: ITI3. Every flag, with its reason, is in [amendment/quality_flags.csv](amendment/quality_flags.csv).
- **The harvest window (8.1):** maturity dates in the sample's 163 regions run from 12 May to 22 August; 63 regions mature before July and 10 after July. Regions where much of the wheat is spring-sown (Finland, Sweden) use the winter-wheat calendar too.
