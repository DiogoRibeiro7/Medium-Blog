# Data: the region-year panel

Generated by `eu-wheat panel` from the crop snapshot `eurostat-crops-2026-10-23`, the harmonised region codes and the E-OBS weather aggregated to NUTS-2021 regions. It describes coverage only: no yield is related to any weather variable here ([METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), section 3).

## Summary

- **Panel:** 3771 region-years in 167 regions of 25 countries, harvest years 2000 to 2024.
- **Codes:** 102 no_match, 27 nuts1_single, 238 nuts2021, 23 recode.
- **Harmonised region-years:** 4864, of which 4463 have a yield. Sources that disagree by more than 1%: 22.
- **Excluded region-years, in the order the rules apply:** no_area_or_production 108; area_below_1kha 849; implausible_yield 8; weather_missing 49; region_below_10_years 79.
- **Countries with a single region in the panel** (no within-country variation for M1): EE, LU, LV.
