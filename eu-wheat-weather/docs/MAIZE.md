# Grain maize: results

Generated by `eu-wheat maize-estimate` from the maize panel ([MAIZE_DATA.md](MAIZE_DATA.md)). Every model, test and decision rule was fixed in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), section 9, before any maize yield was looked at. Details the plan left open were fixed in the code and recorded in its change log afterwards: on 2026-10-26, after the panel was built, and on 2026-10-27, after the estimates. The coefficients are effects of weather shocks given each region's normal and each country's year.

## Placebo (stated first)

**The placebo passes by the registered rule, which needs both methods to reject: one method rejects and the other does not.** What drives the two-way rejection is in the post-hoc section below. Joint test of degree days above 30 °C and precipitation over the 61 days after each region's maturity date: two-way p = 0.043, bootstrap p = 0.158 (by country 0.113, by year 0.158).

Holm over E1 and E2, separately under the two-way method and the bootstrap of the two-way statistic (the larger of its runs by country and by year); established only if rejected under both, and negative.

1893 region-years in 132 regions of 19 countries, harvest years 2010 to 2024; 5 region-years alone in their region or country-year were dropped.

## Limits fixed in advance

- Much of southern Europe's maize is irrigated; the pooled effect averages over irrigated and rainfed maize, and the rainfed calendar is used everywhere.
- Grain maize only; silage maize is not included.
- 15 harvests: no test of a change in sensitivity.

**The irrigated south and elsewhere.** One model with EDD × south (Spain, Portugal, Italy and Greece): -0.1199 [-0.1339, -0.1059] elsewhere and -0.0032 [-0.0241, +0.0177] in the south. **Where the identifying variation comes from** (each country's share of the variable's sum of squares once the fixed effects are removed): `edd` ES 28%, IT 21%, RO 18%; time at 36 °C and above ES 54%, RO 13%, PT 10%; `edd` without the irrigated south RO 42%, FR 21%, BG 13%; `edd` with area weights RO 48%, ES 14%, FR 11%.

**The interval without the irrigated south.** E1's two-way SE there is 0.0076, against 0.0115 (country), 0.0129 (year) and 0.0155 (country-year).

**The placebo's two terms** (two-way): post-maturity degree days above 30 °C +0.0080 [-0.0862, +0.1022], p 0.857, zero in 59% of region-years; post-maturity precipitation (per 100 mm) +0.0143 [+0.0022, +0.0265], p 0.024. Leaving out FR weakens the joint test most (two-way p 0.218). After the fixed effects, post-maturity precipitation correlates -0.003 with the season's. E1 with both terms in the model: -0.0500.

**How far heat moves within a region** (degree days above 30 °C): the median change from one season to the next is 5.4, and 32% of changes reach 10. After the fixed effects the SD is 4.6, 4.6% of region-years lie 10 or more from zero, and 5.2% of the variance of heat is left.

**Hours per season** in the bins above 27 °C: 27-30 °C 208, 30-33 °C 105, 33-36 °C 37, 36 °C and above 9.

**E4's misses by country** (each country's part of the gap between the observed and predicted change, the two largest): 2012: RO -6.6, HU -2.3 of -9.7 points; 2022: HU -4.4, RO -4.4 of -9.9 points.

**Eurostat's estimated values.** MAIZE_DATA.md counts the `e` flags of area and production separately. In the sample, 68 region-years carry one, all in EL, 2016-2024.
