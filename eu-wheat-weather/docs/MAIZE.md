# Grain maize: results

Generated by `eu-wheat maize-estimate` from the maize panel ([MAIZE_DATA.md](MAIZE_DATA.md)). Every model, test and decision rule was fixed in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), section 9, before any maize yield was looked at. The coefficients are effects of weather shocks given each region's normal and each country's year.

## Placebo (stated first)

**The placebo passes by the registered rule, which needs both methods to reject, but only narrowly: one method rejects and the other does not.** Grain maize often stands in the field for weeks after maturity before it is harvested, so weather after maturity can also act on the harvested yield directly. Joint test of degree days above 30 °C and precipitation over the 61 days after each region's maturity date: two-way p = 0.043, bootstrap p = 0.158 (by country 0.113, by year 0.158).

## Decisions

Holm over E1 and E2, separately under the two-way method and the bootstrap of the two-way statistic (the larger of its runs by country and by year); established only if rejected under both, and negative.

| Estimand | Estimate | p, two-way (Holm) | p, bootstrap (Holm) | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| E1, 10 more degree days above 30 °C | -0.0499 (-4.9%) | 0.048 | 0.036 | established negative |
| E2, a dry season (P10 against median) | -0.0338 (-3.3%) | 0.007 | 0.027 | established negative |

## Sample

1893 region-years in 132 regions of 19 countries, harvest years 2010 to 2024; 5 region-years alone in their region or country-year were dropped.

## Limits fixed in advance

- Much of southern Europe's maize is irrigated; the pooled effect averages over irrigated and rainfed maize, and the rainfed calendar is used everywhere.
- Grain maize only; silage maize is not included.
- Fifteen harvests: no test of a change in sensitivity.
