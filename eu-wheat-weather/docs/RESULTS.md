# Results: heat, drought and wheat yields

Generated by `eu-wheat estimate` from the committed panel. Every model, variable, test and decision rule was fixed in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md) before any estimate (sections 4-6 and the change log of 2026-10-24). The coefficients are effects of weather shocks given each region's normal and each country's year, not effects of a changed climate.

## E5, placebo (stated first, as the plan requires)

The placebo passes: post-harvest weather is not significant under both methods. Joint test of post-harvest degree days above 30 °C and precipitation (1 August to 30 September): two-way p = 0.471, wild bootstrap p = 0.709.

## Decisions

Holm over E1-E3, separately under each method; established only if rejected under both, with the required sign.

| Estimand | Estimate | p, two-way (Holm) | p, wild bootstrap (Holm) | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| E1, 10 more degree days above 30 °C | -0.0268 log points (-2.6%) | 0.259 | 0.519 | not determined |
| E2, a dry season (P10 against median) | -0.0231 log points (-2.3%) | 0.006 | 0.002 | established negative |
| E3, change in E1 after 2011 | +0.0119 log points | 0.569 | 0.610 | not determined |

**E2, read with care.** The registered rule takes significance from the joint test of the precipitation terms and the sign from the dry-season effect. The joint test rejects under both methods, but the dry-season effect's own two-way interval, [-0.0536, +0.0074], includes zero. Precipitation clearly matters, while the size of the loss in a typical dry season is not pinned down.

## Sample

- 3693 region-years in 164 regions of 22 countries (468 country-years), harvest years 2000 to 2024.
- 3771 region-years have the weather M1 needs; 78 were alone in their region or country-year and carry no information under M1.

- **E1.** Two-way t = -1.58 (p = 0.130); with country-clustered errors t = -1.71, wild bootstrap p = 0.260.
- **E2.** Joint test of the precipitation terms: two-way p = 0.002, wild bootstrap p = <0.001. A dry season is on average 75 mm below the region's median; it changes log yield by -0.0231 [-0.0536, +0.0074]. Yield peaks at 347 mm over March to July.
- **E3.** The interaction is +0.0119 [-0.0309, +0.0548]: two-way p = 0.569, wild bootstrap p = 0.610. The smallest change this design detects with 80% power is 0.0606 log points per 10 degree days.
- **Second route.** M1 by explicit dummy variables agrees to 2.1e-13.
