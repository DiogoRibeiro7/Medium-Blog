# Data: OMIE's day-ahead order books

Generated by `iberian-lp data` from the downloaded files, as sections 3 and 4 of [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md) fix them. **No programme has been solved, and no curve has been compared with a price; price levels are not shown.** Data: OMIE (OMI-Polo Español, S.A.), omie.es.

- **Files:** 144 (48 days × 3 kinds), 950,049,504 bytes, read with the Modelo de Ficheros para la distribución pública de información, versión 1.37. Names, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in `data/derived/manifest.json`; the files are not committed.
- **Versions:** numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1.
- **Days:** 48 from 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, 4 in each of 12 months, seed 20261028: 2025-10-01, 2025-10-05, 2025-10-06, 2025-10-17, 2025-11-01, 2025-11-02, 2025-11-04, 2025-11-17, 2025-12-03, 2025-12-06, 2025-12-19, 2025-12-30, 2026-01-10, 2026-01-16, 2026-01-21, 2026-01-25, 2026-02-04, 2026-02-06, 2026-02-18, 2026-02-26, 2026-03-03, 2026-03-08, 2026-03-20, 2026-03-28, 2026-04-01, 2026-04-09, 2026-04-11, 2026-04-14, 2026-05-02, 2026-05-11, 2026-05-23, 2026-05-25, 2026-06-01, 2026-06-03, 2026-06-08, 2026-06-30, 2026-07-07, 2026-07-15, 2026-07-20, 2026-07-25, 2026-08-10, 2026-08-12, 2026-08-14, 2026-08-26, 2026-09-03, 2026-09-18, 2026-09-19, 2026-09-27.

## Flows with France

Over the 4,608 joint or Spanish zone-periods: imports from France in 2,533 (median 2,100 MW), exports to France in 2,075 (median 1,600 MW), both in 0.

## Checks

Each compares quantities only, with a tolerance of 0.1 MW.

- **The matched curve balances.** In every zone-period, accepted sell volume plus imports equals accepted buy volume plus exports: largest difference 0.0 MW, 0 zone-periods over the tolerance.
- **The two sides of the Spanish-Portuguese border agree** in split periods: largest difference 0.0 MW, 0 over.
- **The France flows in the curves match the interconnector file** (`capacidad_inter_pbc`, border 3): largest difference 0.0 MW, 0 zone-periods over.

## Reproduce

```
iberian-lp download --raw data/raw
iberian-lp data --raw data/raw --out data/derived --report docs/DATA.md
```
