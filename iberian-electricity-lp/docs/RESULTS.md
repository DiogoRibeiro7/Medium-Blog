# Results: the Iberian price as a linear programme

Generated by `iberian-lp estimates` from `results/programmes.csv`, against the plan in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md) (registered 2026-10-28) and its change log. Data: OMIE (OMI-Polo Español, S.A.), omie.es. Prices are in EUR/MWh.

- **Programmes:** 51,198 solved with HiGHS (SciPy 1.18.1), each checked against its exact dual interval and optimal value (section 4); every check passed.
- **Periods:** 4,608 quarter-hours of 48 days; 5,118 zone-periods, since 510 periods split into Spain and Portugal.
- **Intervals:** percentile bootstrap over days, 9,999 resamples, seed 20261029. Every interval of E2 and E3, including their breakdowns and hourly means, is at 97.5% (Bonferroni over the two questions, not over the hours); the others are 95%. E1's decision uses its estimate.

## E1: the price as a dual (Q1)

The published price π lies in the dual interval Λ_R of the realised programme, widened by 0.01 EUR/MWh at each end, in 100.0% [100.0%, 100.0%] of zone-periods. With every zone-period inside, the bootstrap interval is degenerate and carries no information.

EUPHEMIA, the algorithm that clears the market, must accept every in-the-money period order in full and reject every out-of-the-money one, and it validates its solutions by complementary slackness for its selection of block and complex orders (EUPHEMIA Public Description, 18 December 2025, sections 5.1 and 8.2). `LP_R` fixes that selection, so E1 checks that OMIE's public files and this reconstruction agree; it can fail only through an error in the data or the code ([REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md), finding M1).

| Spain | 510 | 510 |

- **Where it fails:** nowhere.
- **Tolerance of 0.1 EUR/MWh (section 7):** 100.0% [100.0%, 100.0%].
- **Accepted volume:** the published accepted buy volume lies within 0.1 MW of the realised programme's optimal accepted volume in 100.0% [100.0%, 100.0%] of zone-periods (0 do not).
- **Λ_R wider than a point:** 564 zone-periods (11.0%), median width 0.65 EUR/MWh.

## After the results (descriptive, not registered)

Added after the results above had been seen (*results*), or after the critical review (*review*, [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md)); see the change log of [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md). They change no decision. Intervals are 95%.

- **Where G comes from** (*results*). π ≤ 0 in 450 of 1,344 midday periods (33.5%) and in 89 of 3,264 others (2.7%). A sell step at 0 EUR/MWh changes no step's position at prices below zero, so a price at or below zero that clears `LP_R` still clears it with the extra step, and m = 0 in every one of them.
- **The zero floor binds at every price** (*review*). With the extra step, the price never falls below zero (0 exceptions), so |m| ≤ π in every period priced above zero (0 exceptions). The floor binds exactly, with the new price at 0, in 36 of 894 positive midday periods and 23 of 3,175 others, and positive midday prices are lower: median 34.88 against 87.79. So the comparison of the periods priced above zero, mean |m| 7.18 [6.38, 8.03] outside midday against 7.21 [5.39, 9.18] at midday (outside minus midday -0.03 [-1.97, +1.78]), mixes the floor with the slope of the supply curve and does not test the slope.
- **The reason the plan gave** (*review*). Within price bands, mean |m| is larger at midday in 4 of 7. The plan's reason for G > 0, a flatter supply curve at midday, gets no support; G comes from the periods at or below zero.

- **The France line** (*review*). The line had free capacity in the direction of the flow in 1,717 of 4,608 periods (37.3%), where holding the flow fixed is a strong assumption. Mean δ: -10.93 [-12.73, -9.19] with the line full, -13.14 [-14.37, -11.90] with room. Mean m: -5.92 [-6.97, -4.95] and -7.06 [-8.20, -6.02].
- **Exclusive blocks** (*review*). The curves do not say which exclusive blocks are alternatives within one group. Two variants show how much of the blocks-only row comes from counting them as additive supply; neither models a group exactly. With the exclusive blocks deduplicated (of the steps with the same quantity in a period, only the cheapest), mean δ is -0.95 [-1.36, -0.57]; with the exclusive blocks not relaxed, -0.46 [-0.69, -0.26]. With every order relaxed and the exclusive blocks deduplicated, -11.54 [-12.75, -10.31].
- **Joint and split periods** (*review*). Mean δ is -12.37 [-13.61, -11.10] in joint periods, where the relaxation acts on the whole Iberian market, and -6.76 [-11.03, -3.82] in Spain in split periods, where Portugal's orders and the flow between the two stay fixed.
- **The midpoint rule** (*results*). E3's counterfactual interval is wider than a point in 67 of 4,608 periods; in the others the two rules give the same price.
