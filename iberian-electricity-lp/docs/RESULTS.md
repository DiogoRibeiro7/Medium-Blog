# Results: the Iberian price as a linear programme

Generated by `iberian-lp estimates` from `results/programmes.csv`, against the plan in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md) (registered 2026-10-28) and its change log. Data: OMIE (OMI-Polo Español, S.A.), omie.es. Prices are in EUR/MWh.

- **Programmes:** 37,374 solved with HiGHS (SciPy 1.18.1), each checked against its exact dual interval and optimal value (section 4); every check passed.
- **Periods:** 4,608 quarter-hours of 48 days; 5,118 zone-periods, since 510 periods split into Spain and Portugal.
- **Intervals:** percentile bootstrap over days, 9,999 resamples, seed 20261029. Q2 and Q3 decide something, and their intervals are simultaneous (97.5% each, Bonferroni); the others are 95%.

## After the results (descriptive, not registered)

Added after the results above had been seen ([METHODS.md](../METHODS.md), change log). They change no decision. Intervals are 95%.

- **Where G comes from.** π ≤ 0 in 450 of 1,344 midday periods (33.5%) and in 89 of 3,264 others (2.7%). A sell step at 0 EUR/MWh changes no step's position at prices below zero, so a price at or below zero that clears `LP_R` still clears it with the extra step, and m = 0 in every one of them. Over the periods priced above zero, mean |m| is 7.18 [6.38, 8.03] outside midday and 7.21 [5.39, 9.18] at midday, a difference of -0.03 [-1.97, +1.78]; so the registered G comes from the midday periods priced at or below zero; when the price is positive, no difference between midday and the other hours is established.
- **The midpoint rule.** E3's counterfactual interval is wider than a point in 67 of 4,608 periods; in the others the two rules give the same price.
