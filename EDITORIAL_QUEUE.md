## Editorial standard

A post is ready for publication only when it has:

1. one clear question rather than a catalogue of techniques;
2. a claim that can be defended without relying on authority or fashion;
3. explicit separation between observation, modelling assumption, and interpretation;
4. enough mathematical detail to show why the argument works, without turning the article into a paper;
5. reproducible code or a worked example when computation is central to the claim;
6. references for factual or literature-dependent statements;
7. a final pass that removes unsupported causal language and unnecessary jargon.

## Publication states

- **idea** — title or question only;
- **draft** — coherent article exists but evidence, examples, or references are incomplete;
- **review** — content is substantively complete and undergoing technical/editorial review;
- **ready** — suitable for publication;
- **published** — publication URL and date recorded in the project README.
