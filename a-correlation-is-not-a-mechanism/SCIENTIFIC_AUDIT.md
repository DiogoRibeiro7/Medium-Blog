# Scientific audit — Article #35 (synthetic evidence)

## Scope and archive contract

The nine scenarios are simulations, not empirical findings. Each scenario has 300 independent pseudo-random replicates generated by `numpy.random.SeedSequence([20261102, study_index])`, spawning one child stream per replicate. The archive comprises `data/<study>_replicates.csv` and `data/<study>_summary.csv` for all nine studies, plus a PNG for each summary. The code refuses to write into the repository without an explicit output directory.

The test suite regenerates the complete archive and checks every row, field, and categorical identifier. Floating-point comparisons use absolute and relative tolerances of `1e-12` for BLAS/platform differences. These tolerances are numerical, not uncertainty intervals.

Each table distinguishes the *sampling SD* of individual estimates from the *MCSE* of the mean across the 300 replications. The figure's whiskers show ±1.96 MCSE. They are not 95% confidence intervals for a single dataset.

## Assumptions and interpretation safeguards

1. In the first study, the adjustment variable is observed, temporally prior, measured without error and sufficient to block confounding; the naive slope formula assumes unit-variance treatment noise.
2. Simpson's reversal is produced by treatment propensity and baseline-risk composition. The observed reversal alone does not prove any real treatment effect.
3. Collider and backdoor results assume linear Gaussian structural equations, independent disturbances and no measurement error. Conditioning on descendants can behave differently in other graphs.
4. The mediation model has randomized exposure and no mediator-outcome confounding. The reported regression on T and M estimates a controlled direct effect in this additive model, not an automatically identified natural direct effect.
5. Instrumental variables require relevance, independence and exclusion; the Gaussian continuous setup illustrates a structural linear IV coefficient, **not** an unrestricted LATE claim.
6. IPW uses the true propensity by construction. Near-violated positivity is not repaired by knowing propensity perfectly; the finite-sample variance rises.
7. Observational equivalence is an exact statement about the bivariate Gaussian joint law: both parameterizations share means and covariance, yet have different interventional effects.
8. Proxy manipulation is defined as modifying the measurement X alone, leaving its latent parent U unchanged. This distinction is essential when talking about feature importance and policies.
