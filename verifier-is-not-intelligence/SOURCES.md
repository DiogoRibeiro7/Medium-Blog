# Evidence ledger

Accessed 2026-11-28. All research claims should be interpreted in the settings of the corresponding study. The mathematical scenarios use no data from these sources.

| Source | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [DeepSeek-R1, Nature (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09422-z) | Rule-based and learned rewards, and limitations in reward reliability | Not proof that other domains cannot be learned |
| [AlphaProof, Nature (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09833-y) | Formal theorem proving using Lean and RL | Formal specification is supplied; search and formalization remain challenges |
| [Yue et al., NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/537d5aa768c2d534016a4d06f87bc8fb-Abstract-Conference.html) | RLVR improves low-k sampling; studied settings show limits at high k | No universal ceiling on reasoning or all future RL |
| [Cheng et al., 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14965) | Six verifiable domains, heterogeneous cross-domain transfer | Narrow tasks, not whole professions |
| [Pyatkin et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02833) | Verifiable instruction constraints outside mathematics and programming | Constraint satisfaction is not overall response usefulness |
| [Yin et al., 2026 preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15464v2) | Controlled comparison of RLVR and RLHF across domains | Preliminary, not a universal ranking of methods |
| [Yu et al., 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22074) | Outcome rewards need not improve causal importance or sufficiency of reasoning | Specific models and tasks; auxiliary rewards help in experiments |
| [Gao et al., 2022/2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10760) | Controlled reward-model overoptimization experiments | Proxy-model experiment, not direct estimates of our toy masses |
| [Skalse et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13085) | Formal characterization of unhackable reward pairs under stated assumptions | Not an impossibility theorem for useful narrow reward design |
| [Dell'Acqua et al., Organization Science (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838) | Randomized consulting experiment, uneven gains and failures | GPT-4-era study, not a current-model ceiling |
| [METR, July 2025](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) and [February 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) | RCT slowdown in early 2025, later measurement confounded by selection | Not proof of a universal or permanent slowdown |
| [METR, time-horizon methodology](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) | Evidence of improvements on scoped tasks, human-duration-based reliability metric | Not continuous autonomous runtime or every occupation |
| [ILO, 2025 occupational exposure](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure) | Task exposure and potential transformation | Not observed displacement or guaranteed employment forecast |
| [International AI Safety Report 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026) | Documented capabilities and misuse, emerging loss-of-control evidence and substantial uncertainty | Neither a demonstration of extinction nor proof that catastrophic risk is zero |

The quoted inspiration was supplied by the author without a source URL or name. No wording, enumerated accusations, or personal claims from it are used as evidence. The article develops an independent verifier/decision-theory argument with primary citations.
