== Worked cases
mammography screening (Gigerenzer et al. 2007): base rate 1%, sensitivity 90%, false-positive rate 9%
  out of 1,000: 10 positive, 9.0 of them flagged; 990 negative, 89.1 of them flagged; 98.1 flags, 9.2% right
Eddy (1982): base rate 1%, sensitivity 80%, false-positive rate 9.6%
  share of positives that are right: 7.8%
face matching, Champions League final, Cardiff 2017 (South Wales Police figures)
  2,470 alerts, 173 right, 2,297 wrong: 7.0% right; the wrong alerts are 1.35% of a crowd of 170,000
OpenAI's text classifier (January 2023): flags 26% of AI-written text and 9% of human text
  if 3% of essays are AI-written: out of 1,000: 30 positive, 7.8 of them flagged; 970 negative, 87.3 of them flagged; 95.1 flags, 8.2% right
  if 11% of essays are AI-written: out of 1,000: 110 positive, 28.6 of them flagged; 890 negative, 80.1 of them flagged; 108.7 flags, 26.3% right
a detector with a 1% false-positive rate (Turnitin's stated bound, full documents), 3% AI-written:
  false-positive rate 1.0%: right 60.7%, 69.9%, 75.6% for sensitivity 50%, 75%, 100%
  false-positive rate 0.5%: right 75.6%, 82.3%, 86.1% for sensitivity 50%, 75%, 100%
card payments: 0.015% fraudulent by number (EEA, first half of 2023); detector catching 90%
  false-positive rate 1.00%: 1.3% of alerts right, 74.1 false alerts per fraud
  false-positive rate 0.10%: 11.9% of alerts right, 7.4 false alerts per fraud
  false-positive rate 0.01%: 57.5% of alerts right, 0.7 false alerts per fraud

== A real card-fraud model (ULB and Worldline data, September 2013)
284,807 transactions, 492 frauds (0.173%), over two days
  day 1: 144,786 transactions, 281 frauds (0.194%)
  day 2: 140,021 transactions, 211 frauds (0.151%)
logistic regression trained on day 1, scored on day 2: AUC 0.958, average precision 0.643
at the data's fraud rate (0.151%) and at the EEA rate for card payments (0.015%), same detector:
  catching 50%: 147 alerts, 106 frauds, 41 false (false-positive rate 0.029%); right 72.1%, 0.4 false per fraud | at the EEA rate: right 20.4%, 4 false per fraud
  catching 60%: 182 alerts, 127 frauds, 55 false (false-positive rate 0.039%); right 69.8%, 0.4 false per fraud | at the EEA rate: right 18.7%, 4 false per fraud
  catching 70%: 281 alerts, 148 frauds, 133 false (false-positive rate 0.095%); right 52.7%, 0.9 false per fraud | at the EEA rate: right 10.0%, 9 false per fraud
  catching 80%: 954 alerts, 169 frauds, 785 false (false-positive rate 0.561%); right 17.7%, 4.6 false per fraud | at the EEA rate: right 2.1%, 47 false per fraud
  catching 90%: 12,368 alerts, 190 frauds, 12,178 false (false-positive rate 8.710%); right 1.5%, 64.1 false per fraud | at the EEA rate: right 0.2%, 645 false per fraud

== What a flag needs
base rate 11.000%, sensitivity 90%: false-positive rate at most 11.124% for half the flags to be right, 1.236% for 90%, 0.1124% for 99%
base rate 3.000%, sensitivity 90%: false-positive rate at most 2.784% for half the flags to be right, 0.309% for 90%, 0.0281% for 99%
base rate 1.000%, sensitivity 90%: false-positive rate at most 0.909% for half the flags to be right, 0.101% for 90%, 0.0092% for 99%
base rate 0.015%, sensitivity 90%: false-positive rate at most 0.014% for half the flags to be right, 0.002% for 90%, 0.0001% for 99%
sensitivity / false-positive rate needed for 90% right flags at 3.000%: 291
sensitivity / false-positive rate needed for 90% right flags at 0.015%: 59,991
the average detector: sensitivity / false-positive rate 9.3 on native essays, 0.79 on non-native essays; OpenAI's classifier 2.9; Cardiff, if every one of 170,000 faces was checked once and all were innocent but 173: at most 74

== Reading a score and acting on a flag
a score calibrated on a balanced test set, read where 3% are positive: 90% -> 21.8%, 98% -> 60.2%, 99.9% -> 96.9%
a score calibrated on a balanced test set, read where 11% are positive: 90% -> 52.7%, 98% -> 85.8%, 99.9% -> 99.2%
a false alarm costing 1 times a miss: act when a flag is right with probability above 50.0%
a false alarm costing 20 times a miss: act when a flag is right with probability above 95.2%
a false alarm costing 0.025 times a miss: act when a flag is right with probability above 2.4%
mammography, a second positive from an independent test of the same accuracy: 9.2% -> 50.3%
