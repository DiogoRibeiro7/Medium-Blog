1. EXACT ENUMERATION (player holds door 1)
========================================================================
  car behind 1, host opens 2: probability 1/6
  car behind 1, host opens 3: probability 1/6
  car behind 2, host opens 3: probability 1/3
  car behind 3, host opens 2: probability 1/3
  P(stay wins)                       1/3 = 0.333333
  P(always switch wins)              2/3 = 0.666667
  P(car behind 2 | host opened 3)    2/3 = 0.666667
  likelihood ratio P(open 3|car 2)/P(open 3|car 1) = 2
  posterior odds door 2 : door 1 = 1 x 2 = 2:1

  Host with a preference (Morgan et al. 1991): opens door 3 w.p. q when free to choose
    q =    0:  P(win switch | opened 3) =    1   | opened 2) =  1/2   unconditional = 2/3
    q =  1/4:  P(win switch | opened 3) =  4/5   | opened 2) =  4/7   unconditional = 2/3
    q =  1/2:  P(win switch | opened 3) =  2/3   | opened 2) =  2/3   unconditional = 2/3
    q =  3/4:  P(win switch | opened 3) =  4/7   | opened 2) =  4/5   unconditional = 2/3
    q =    1:  P(win switch | opened 3) =  1/2   | opened 2) =    1   unconditional = 2/3
  closed forms: 1/(1+q) after door 3, 1/(2-q) after door 2; never below 1/2

  Ignorant host (opens 2 or 3 at random, a goat happens to appear): 1/2 = 0.500000
  Bertrand's boxes, P(gold-gold | gold seen):                       2/3 = 0.666667
  Three prisoners, P(A pardoned | warden names B) = 1/3,  P(C pardoned | B named) = 2/3

  Literal simulation, 1,000,000 games: stay 0.3327, switch 0.6673

3a. MONTY HALL'S DINING-ROOM EXPERIMENT (NYT, 21 July 1991)
========================================================================
  stay: 4/10 cars    switch: 8/10 cars
  Bayes factor, (1/3, 2/3) against (1/2, 1/2): 4.927  (exactly 2^34/3^20 = 4.927)
    from the switch arm alone: 4.439;  from the stay arm alone: 1.110
    posterior P(vos Savant's model) from 50:50 prior odds: 0.831
  Fisher exact test, one-sided (switch better): p = 0.0849
  Binomial test of 8/10 against 1/2, one-sided: p = 0.0547  (exactly 56/1024)
  95% Clopper-Pearson interval,   stay: [0.122, 0.738]
  95% Clopper-Pearson interval, switch: [0.444, 0.975]
  P(switch - stay >= 4) if both win half the time: 0.0577
  P(switch - stay >= 4) under vos Savant's model:  0.4793
  under vos Savant's model, 10 + 10 games give switch ahead 0.9081, tie 0.0543, stay ahead 0.0376
  ten switching games under the 2/3 model: median 7 wins, Bayes factor 2.22; P(at least 8 wins) = 0.2991
  expected log10 Bayes factor after ten switching games: 0.246 (factor 1.76)
  stay rate at which ten stays favour neither model: ln(4/3)/ln(2) = 0.4150
