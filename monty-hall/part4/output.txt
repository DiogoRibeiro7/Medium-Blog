1. WHAT EXPERIENCE CAN TEACH
============================================================================
  When the car is revealed at the end of a game, the player learns whether switching would have
  won, whatever was chosen. Each game is a Bernoulli trial with P(switching wins) = 2/3.
  A learner who ignores the reasoning and switches whenever switching has won more of the past
  games than staying, and tosses a coin when they are tied (including the first game):
    after   1 games: P(switch next game) = 0.667

    after   3 games: P(switch next game) = 0.741

    after  30 games: P(switch next game) = 0.969
    after  50 games: P(switch next game) = 0.992

2. BELIEF AND DECISION
============================================================================
  Prior: probability pi0 that the game is exactly fifty-fifty, otherwise P(switching wins) uniform.
  Predictive P(switching wins next game) = pi * 1/2 + (1 - pi) * (k + 1)/(n + 2), which exceeds 1/2
  exactly when k > n/2: the decision does not depend on pi0, the conviction does.
    pi0 = 0.5 : share with P(not fifty-fifty) > 0.95 after  n=10: 0.02; n=20: 0.06; n=50: 0.26; n=100: 0.60
    pi0 = 0.9 : share with P(not fifty-fifty) > 0.95 after  n=10: 0.00; n=20: 0.02; n=50: 0.10; n=100: 0.35
    pi0 = 0.99: share with P(not fifty-fifty) > 0.95 after  n=10: 0.00; n=20: 0.00; n=50: 0.03; n=100: 0.15
    pi0 = 0.5 : games until half of learners are convinced 85; until 90 per cent are 175
    pi0 = 0.9 : games until half of learners are convinced 127; until 90 per cent are 234
    pi0 = 0.99: games until half of learners are convinced 172; until 90 per cent are 291
  decision 'switch iff k > n/2' identical for all pi0 checked (n = 1..59): True

4. REINFORCEMENT LEARNERS
============================================================================
  Delta-rule learners, P(switch) = expit(beta * (Q_switch - Q_stay) - bias), Q starting at 0.5.
  A bias b gives a first-game switch rate of expit(-b): b = ln 9 gives 10 per cent.
    beta =  3, b = ln 9: asymptotic switch rate 0.232
    beta =  6, b = ln 9: asymptotic switch rate 0.451
    beta = 10, b = ln 9: asymptotic switch rate 0.757
    switch rate 2/3 at the asymptote with b = 0 requires beta = 3 ln 2 = 2.079
    with b = ln 9, beta for an asymptote of 2/3: 8.671; of 0.9: 13.183

  Learning from the outcome of the door not chosen (full) against from one's own outcome only (own):
    alpha 0.03, beta 10.0, full: switch rate in games 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 = 0.100, 0.223, 0.407, 0.580, 0.686, 0.710; first game above one half: 36

5. REGRET FOR AN ACT OF COMMISSION
============================================================================
  Payoff of a loss after switching = -kappa (a loss after staying = 0).
  Expected payoff: switching 2/3 - kappa/3, staying 1/3; staying is better when kappa > 1.
    kappa 0.0: switch +0.667, stay +0.333; asymptotic switch rate at beta 10, b = ln 9: 0.757; at beta 10, b = 0: 0.966
    kappa 0.5: switch +0.500, stay +0.333; asymptotic switch rate at beta 10, b = ln 9: 0.370; at beta 10, b = 0: 0.841
    kappa 1.0: switch +0.333, stay +0.333; asymptotic switch rate at beta 10, b = ln 9: 0.100; at beta 10, b = 0: 0.500
