# Sources of the transcribed tables

All files in this folder are transcribed by hand from public U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics releases (U.S. government works, public domain). Each value was checked against two renderings of the same release (the HTML table and the PDF release).

## `nlsy79_table2_vintages.csv`

Table 2, "Duration of employment relationships with a single employer ... by age at start of job", National Longitudinal Survey of Youth 1979 (cohort born 1957–64). Only the total row of each age-at-start group is transcribed. Columns `lt1` … `lt15` are the cumulative percent of jobs that ended in less than 1, 2, 5, 10 and 15 years; `ongoing` is the percent of jobs still in progress at the survey date. Empty cells are cells BLS did not publish.

`phrasing` records how the release text describes the statistic: `jobs` for releases that write "X percent of those jobs ended in less than a year" (2015, 2017, 2019) and `average worker` for releases that write "the average worker had X percent of those jobs end in less than a year" (2021, 2023, 2025). None of the releases states that the method changed.

- 2015-03-31: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_03312015.htm
- 2017-08-24: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_08242017.htm
- 2019-08-22: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_08222019.htm
- 2021-08-31: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_08312021.htm
- 2023-08-22: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_08222023.htm
- 2025-08-26: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/nlsoy_08262025.htm (current table: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/nlsoy.t02.htm)

## `nlsy97_table2_2026.csv`

Table 2 of the NLSY97 release of 2026-05-05 (cohort born 1980–84), total rows only: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/nlsyth.t02.htm

## `cps_median_tenure.csv`

Table 1 of "Employee Tenure in 2026" (released 2026-09-24), median years with the current employer, employed wage and salary workers, both sexes, Current Population Survey January supplements: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/tenure.t01.htm
