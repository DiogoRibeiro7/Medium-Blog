Outcome variable: 
- Cartelperiod (binary)

Screens: 
- cvbid denotes the coefficient of variation
- Diffperc denotes the percentage difference
- Kolmo_stat denotes the Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic
- Spread denotes the spread
- RDnorm denotes the normalized distance
- D denotes the absolute difference
- skew1 denotes the skewness
- RDalter denotes the alternative distance

Summary statistics: 
- min3 denotes the min (of a coalition of three firms)
- max3 denotes the max
- mean3 denotes the mean
- median3 denotes the median
- P5 denotes the 5th percentile 
	(identical for the summary statistics P10, P25, P75, P90, and P95)

Additional variables: 
- Country (Switzerland, Italy, Japan)
- ID of the coalition: idfirm
